## The competition

An international student competition to design, build, and fly an eVTOL UAS, then pitch it to investors. Each team picks one track and competes within a single year.

| Score | Weight | What it is |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fly-off | 40% | A live mission in the team's track, streamed. Scored against the best run in the same track |
| Entrepreneurial pitch | 35% | A live pitch to investors. A season-long founder curriculum is provided |
| Design report | 25% | Up to 25 pages, scored out of 500 against the track rubric: trade studies, sizing, autonomy, safety, test results |

**Tracks:** I. Infrastructure Inspection · II. Urban Medical Transport · III. Precision Agriculture.

**Venue:** Infrastructure Inspection and Urban Medical Transport fly at the Texas A&M RELLIS Campus. Precision Agriculture flies at the Brazos Bottom Research Farm. Every team pitches on the main campus in College Station.

**Prize:** $10,000 pool sponsored by Bray International, across the top three overall, the best team in each track, and the best fly-off, pitch, and design report.

**Fees:** none listed. The team portal says "No fee."

## Eligibility

- Teams from any school can apply.
- At least half of each team must be full-time students.
- A faculty or doctoral advisor.
- **A Part 107 certified pilot.** See [FAA compliance](faa-compliance.md): the plan already calls for two to four members to hold Part 107.
